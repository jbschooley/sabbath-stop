When are you leaving?

Every arrival time depends on this. It is the clock time at your starting point: the same as your device when you start from where you are, otherwise the start's own zone. Defaults to the current time on a Sunday, otherwise 8:00 AM next Sunday, and remembers a future time you set. A Google Maps link with "depart at" or an ABRP export fills it in; Apple Maps links carry no time.

h min

The free router has no traffic data. If your navigation app predicts a different time for this departure, enter it and every arrival and detour is scaled to match. An ABRP export sets each leg's time itself and ignores this field.

Route

      Each stop can carry a dwell time, so a 40-minute charge pushes every later arrival back 40 minutes.

      The link's places are copied into the A → B tab, where you can adjust stops and dwell times. Tesla Superchargers get 15 minutes by default; an ABRP export link brings its own charge times and per-leg drive times. A link with no start uses your location. Short maps.app.goo.gl links can't be expanded here: open the link, then paste the full URL from the address bar.

      An ABRP "Export to Excel" file is copied into the A → B tab: each stop by name, charging time as dwell, and ABRP's own per-leg drive times. It has no coordinates, so check each stop's suggestion. GPX tracks with timestamps are used as-is; tracks without, and KML, are re-routed through their shape.
